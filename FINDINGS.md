# Prikk Findings and Risk Register

**Authority.** This file is the project's risk and finding register. The **architect maintains it**:
`.git-exclude/roles/high-capability-model-operating-instructions.md` requires the architect to
"actively identify and track" risk (line 273), to document significant risk including its residual
(line 290), and to never "silently accept significant risk" (line 300). Recording or updating a row
here needs no separate authorization.

**The developer does not write here.** Findings arising from implementation or investigation are
reported in the review-request or investigation document, and the architect records them. Reporting a
finding and registering it are two different acts.

**Nothing here schedules anything.** The `Milestone` column *references* `MILESTONES.md`'s labels;
assigning or changing a milestone is the project owner's, as is any release condition. A row saying
`Unowned` means recorded, visible, and available for scheduling — not scheduled, and not accepted as
closed.

**Provenance.** This register was split out of `MILESTONES.md` on 2026-08-08, verbatim and unedited.
`MILESTONES.md` is titled "Corrective Milestones" and opens "This file schedules the corrective
program", yet had carried this register inside it — a scheduling document holding content with
different authorship. Two agents misread the boundary from that structure on the same day, one of them
the architect. Documents written before that date may refer to "`MILESTONES.md`'s finding-ownership
table"; those records are accurate as of their own date and are deliberately left unrewritten.
